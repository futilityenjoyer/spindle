Static sheen, rotating labelGrooves are radially symmetric, so they live in a static layer together with the anisotropic highlight. Only the label (and a 2–3 % pressing irregularity) rotates — no shimmer from rotating fine rings.
InertiaSpin-up: first-order lag, τ = 0.22 s (≈95 % speed in 0.65 s). Spin-down: viscous + friction drag, stops in ≈1.6 s. Track change crossfades the label in 450 ms without touching rotation.
Artwork rules“Inscribed” keeps the full square art (4 pt corners, nothing over it) on a label coloured from the art’s edge pixels. “Still” is the same label held upright and motionless — only the disc and run-out etching turn. “Full-bleed” crops to a circle — shown for comparison, flagged non-compliant with Spotify’s artwork guidance.
Clear & TintedDisc is drawn as a luminance-to-alpha “glass record”; album art stays full colour (HIG explicitly allows full-colour album art). Toggle “Art in colour” off to preview the accented-desaturated fallback.
All artwork, titles and artists are fictional and generated in-page. The circle-and-dot mark is an attribution placeholder only — use the official Spotify logo files in the shipping app. Widgets on iOS cannot run continuous animation with public API; the spin shown on widgets here illustrates the in-app feel and the optional live-spin experiment.
